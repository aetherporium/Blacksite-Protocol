# Assets

**Art direction:** Cinematic tactical realism with industrial brutalism. The arena is rain-wet blue-black concrete and oxidized steel, punctured by white tungsten work lamps and minimal Signal Vermilion warnings. Visual clarity and PBR-like material cues matter more than raw asset count.
