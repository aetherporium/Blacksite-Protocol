# Quality Upgrade Plan — Blind Comparison Round 1

## Objective

The blind critic chose the public AAA reference by a large margin. Blacksite Protocol will not attempt to copy proprietary content or claim parity with a whole AAA production. This pass instead closes the most visible gaps in the browser vertical slice: **first-person motion, weapon silhouette and feedback, enemy threat presentation, depth composition, surface response, and late-combat evidence**.

## Upgrade Contract

| System | Change | Visible acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| Player motion | Add velocity-based acceleration/deceleration, sprint FOV, cadence-driven bob, landing/step sway, and a smooth camera recoil spring. | The camera feels grounded rather than sliding; sprint visibly changes pace and field of view. |
| Weapon | Expand the viewmodel into recognizable receiver, grip, magazine, optic, barrel, suppressor/shroud, and animated muzzle/impact effects. | The rifle reads as a purposeful tactical weapon at a glance, not a dark primitive. |
| Enemies | Differentiate three procedural archetypes by armor, helmet, optic, shoulder mass, pose, and position; add readable firing telemetry and stronger impact response. | At least three hostile silhouettes read as intentionally different threats with scale and depth. |
| Environment | Increase visible wetness, structural layering, industrial decals, utility detail, light pools, and contrast around the combat corridor. | Foreground, midground, and distant industrial depth remain legible in the live frame. |
| Demo proof | Advance the deterministic capture to a late-combat composition with hostile fire, tracers, muzzle flash, and staggered enemy positions. | The demo screenshot communicates an active FPS encounter, not a static test range. |

## Boundaries

The pass avoids imported/copyrighted character or weapon models. It uses original procedural geometry, materials, generated in-project art direction, and browser-safe effects. The result will be evaluated again with an isolated still-image judge, while movement and combat changes will be verified through deterministic gameplay behavior and code-level checks rather than a false claim that a screenshot can prove them.
